Allow microphone for Daari

You only ever have to do this once.

Chrome attaches microphone permission to whichever website asks for it. If Daari asked from inside each site you visit, you would get this prompt again and again, on every website. Asking here instead means the permission belongs to Daari itself, and you will never be asked again.

Daari listens only while you are holding the microphone button. It never listens in the background, and it never clicks or types anything for you.

Do not want to use your voice? You can close this tab and type your goal into the Daari panel instead. Everything works the same way.
